## General rule
At Buddhist temples, Hindu kovils, mosques and most religious sites in Sri Lanka, both shoulders
and knees should be covered - no sleeveless tops, shorts, or short skirts. This applies to all
visitors regardless of gender.

## Footwear and headwear
Shoes and socks must be removed before entering most temple grounds, not just the inner shrine -
at major sites like the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic (Sri Dalada Maligawa) in Kandy, this
means the entire temple complex. Bring a bag to carry your shoes, and consider socks for hot
stone/tile walkways at midday. Head coverings are generally not required for Buddhist temples but
are expected at some mosques (for women) and are sometimes required to be removed for men.

## Photography
Do not turn your back to a Buddha statue for a photo (posing with your back to a Buddha image is
considered highly disrespectful) - this has led to prosecutions of tourists in the past. Ask
before photographing worshippers.
