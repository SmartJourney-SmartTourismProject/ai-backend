## Two monsoons, opposite coasts
Sri Lanka has two monsoon seasons that affect different parts of the island at different times,
which is why there is usually good weather somewhere on the island year-round.

## Yala monsoon (southwest)
Roughly May to September, bringing rain mainly to the southwest and hill country (Colombo, Galle,
the south coast, Kandy). The east coast (Trincomalee, Arugam Bay) tends to be drier and sunnier
during this period.

## Maha monsoon (northeast)
Roughly October to January (peaking November-December), bringing rain mainly to the north and
east (Jaffna, Trincomalee, Arugam Bay). The southwest coast and hill country tend to be drier
during this period.

## Practical planning
Because the two coasts are affected at different times, a west/south-coast beach trip is
generally best outside May-September, while an east-coast trip (Trincomalee, Arugam Bay) is
generally best outside October-January. Rain in Sri Lanka is often heavy but short - a wet season
does not mean rain all day, every day.
