## The custom
In Sri Lanka, as in much of South Asia, the left hand is traditionally considered unclean (used
for personal hygiene) and the right hand is used for eating, giving/receiving items, and touching
people or religious objects.

## Practical guidance
- Eat with your right hand where food is served without cutlery (common with rice and curry).
- Hand money, cards, or gifts to someone with your right hand, or both hands together as a mark
  of respect - not the left hand alone.
- This is a soft social norm, not a hard rule that visitors will be sharply corrected on, but
  using the right hand where practical is appreciated and shows awareness of local custom.
