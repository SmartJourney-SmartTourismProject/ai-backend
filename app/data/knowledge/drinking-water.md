## Tap water
Tap water in Sri Lanka is generally not considered safe for travelers to drink directly, even in
Colombo. Stick to bottled or properly filtered/boiled water for drinking, and for brushing teeth
if you are being cautious.

## Bottled water
Sealed bottled water is widely available and inexpensive. Check that the seal is intact when
buying from small roadside shops. Ice in reputable hotels and restaurants that cater to tourists
is usually made from purified water, but is worth asking about at very informal food stalls.

## Reducing plastic waste
Sri Lanka has a growing number of places offering filtered water refill stations (many
eco-lodges and some hotels) - bringing a reusable bottle and using these where available reduces
plastic waste versus buying bottled water at every stop.
