## National emergency numbers
- Police emergency: 119
- Ambulance / Suwa Seriya (free national ambulance service): 1990
- Tourist Police: 1912 (English-speaking, set up specifically to assist visitors with theft,
  scams, and other tourist-related issues)
- Fire and rescue: 110

## When to use the Tourist Police
The Tourist Police (1912) is the most useful first call for a visitor dealing with theft, a
transport dispute, being overcharged, or other tourist-targeted issues - they are set up to
handle these specifically and are more likely to have English-speaking officers than a general
station.

## Embassy contact
Save your own country's embassy or consulate contact details in Colombo before you need them -
requirements for lost passports and other emergencies vary by nationality.
