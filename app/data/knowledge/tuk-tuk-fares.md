## Metered vs negotiated
Few street-hailed tuk-tuks in Sri Lanka run their meter honestly for tourists. The reliable way to
get a fair, fixed price is a ride-hailing app - PickMe and Uber both operate in Sri Lanka's main
cities and tourist areas and show the price before you book, with no bargaining needed.

## If negotiating directly
If hailing a tuk-tuk on the street, agree the full fare BEFORE getting in, in the local currency
(LKR), and be specific about the destination. A rough guide many travelers use is roughly LKR
80-120 per kilometer within a city for a bargained fare, but this varies by area and season and
should not be relied on as a fixed rate - ask your hotel what a fair local price looks like for a
specific route.

## Safety
Prefer three-wheelers with a visible registration/meter and a driver who seems familiar with the
area. Keep valuables close, and confirm the fare again before starting to move if there was any
ambiguity.
