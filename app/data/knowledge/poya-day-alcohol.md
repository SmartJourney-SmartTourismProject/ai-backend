## What a Poya day is
Every full moon day (Poya) is a Buddhist religious observance day and a public holiday in Sri
Lanka. There are 12-13 Poya days a year, one per lunar month.

## The alcohol ban
On Poya days, the sale of alcohol is restricted nationwide by law - most liquor shops, bars and
many restaurants stop serving or selling alcohol for the day. Some licensed hotels and
tourist-oriented establishments may still serve alcohol to registered guests, but this varies and
should not be assumed. Plan ahead if a Poya day falls during your trip: buy what you need the day
before, or check with your hotel directly.

## Other closures
Many government offices and some shops also close or reduce hours on Poya days. Meat sales are
also traditionally restricted on Poya days in many areas.
