## Who needs an ETA
Most foreign nationals visiting Sri Lanka for tourism need an Electronic Travel Authorization
(ETA) before arrival, applied for online at the official government site eta.gov.lk. Citizens of
a small number of countries are visa-exempt or have other arrangements - check the official site
for your nationality rather than assuming.

## How to apply
Apply only at eta.gov.lk. Several unofficial third-party sites charge a large markup for the same
service and are not affiliated with the Sri Lankan government - the official ETA fee is
substantially cheaper. Processing is usually fast (often within a day), but apply a few days
before departure to allow for delays.

## At the airport
Bring a printed or digital copy of your ETA approval, your passport (valid for the required
minimum period after arrival - check the current requirement on the official site), and proof of
onward/return travel. Immigration officers may ask for these at Bandaranaike International
Airport (Colombo).
